Why this is cheap to build
  • No new data. deriveTasks(), deriveIssueStatus() and deriveStageStates() already produce every field on the Desk — they power the issue card and /my-tasks today.
  • No new routes. The root redirect changes target; /issues keeps its current page unchanged.
  • No pipeline change. Nothing here touches an agent, a schema, or a Convex table.
  • The rails are one grid line. The Draft stage’s two rails come from a single grid-cols-[232px_1fr_320px] in the workspace frame — the story mockup was always rails-free.
