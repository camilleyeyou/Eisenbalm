<objective>
Rename obscure operator-facing labels on the Gate-1 "Needs your decision" card to plain
language, extending the Phase 47 D-07 precedent ("Needs your decision" instead of the
internal `requiresHumanInput` flag) to the rest of the card's copy.

The client (via the product owner) flagged four labels/messages as jargon:
- "Rationale (required)" reads as legalese; operators do not know the reason is audit-only.
- "Burden" and "Evidence quality" are internal verification vocabulary.
- "Adjudication failed." is opaque when submitting a choice errors out.

Purpose: This is the operator dashboard (Mission Control), not the public magazine — the
copy must be plain, helpful English, NOT Jesse's editorial voice. Clear labels remove
confusion at the one human decision gate in the weekly pipeline.

Output: Two component files with renamed copy (the decision card + the signal-desk panel it
was adapted from, for the exact shared error/success strings) and one updated test file.
</objective>
