Every display that reads `parseCostJson(run.cost).total` therefore shows $0. This includes RunDetail's reconciliation panel (~L600-625), RunsTable (L136), ReviewQueue, and CostRollup (`sumRunsCost`). The durable per-step cost is `agent_runs.costUsd`. `deriveRunCostUsd(rows)` in lib/derivedState.ts (L660) already sums it.

- `aggregateStatus` marks the step 'failed'.

The live variants (writer → rerollAgent, publisher → publishManual, Improve → Link to /admin/agents/{key}) work and stay.

"Seven writing agents" (bug 7): lib/nomenclature.ts L92-98 hardcodes `agentLabel: 'seven writing agents'` for the 7 writer keys. The RunDetail draft group lists only the writer rows actually present (often 6).
