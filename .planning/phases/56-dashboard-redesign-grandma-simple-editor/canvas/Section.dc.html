Kwabena Owusu-Ansah Has Never Called Himself a Founder

A retired surveyor, a four-page list, and the sale of one motorbike.

Click any paragraph to change the words. Changes save to this issue straight away.

Kwabena Owusu-Ansah spent thirty-one years measuring land for the Lands Commission in Kumasi. He retired in 2013 with a pension, a motorbike, and a habit of noticing roofs.

The habit became a list. On weekend rides through the Ashanti Region he began writing down every school where the state had poured a slab, raised block walls and then stopped before the trusses. By the end of the first year the list ran to four pages.

Nothing goes to readers until you press Publish on the issue page.

Mr Owusu-Ansah does not give interviews about himself. Asked why, he says the roofs are the interview, and that they are available for inspection during school hours.

He is seventy-one. He still rides to every site before the first sheet goes up, and again after the last one.
